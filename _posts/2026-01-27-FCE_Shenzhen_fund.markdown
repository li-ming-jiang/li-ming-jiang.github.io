## Participated Shenzhen Key R&D Fund
We have been informed that the Shenzhen Key R&D fund Industry application scheme has been awarded. The project is to develop firefighting robots for high-rise building fire scenarios. The total fund from Shenzhen ITC is 5 million HKD and PolyU will receive 0.5 million HKD.
