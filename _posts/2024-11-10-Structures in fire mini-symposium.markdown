## Structures in fire MiniSymposium in 1st International Conference on Engineering Structures
We have led and held the 'Structures in fire' mini-symposium in the 1st International Conference on Engineering Structures. This is the 1st international conference held by the prestigous journal "Engineering Structures", and we organnized the only fire safety engineering related mini-symposium in this conference. Dr Liming Jiang is the lead organiser, and the co-organizers are Prof Asif Usmani, Prof David Lange from UQ, and Dr Shaojun Zhu from Tongji University.
