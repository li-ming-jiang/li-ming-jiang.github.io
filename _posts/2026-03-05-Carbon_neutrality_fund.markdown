## PolyU Carbon Neutrality Fund Awarded
We are glad to share that PolyU Carbon Neutrality Fund Award has been awarded. The project is to develop timber-integrated designs powered by 3DCP to PolyU campus as demonstration of innovative and low-carbon construction solutions. The fund in total is 2 million, which includes 1.9 million from PolyU Central and 0.1 million from departmental matching.
