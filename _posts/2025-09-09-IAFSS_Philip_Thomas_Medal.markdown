## Prestigious Award--IAFSS Philip Thomas Medal of Excellence

We are thrilled to receive this prestigious award of IAFSS: Philip Thomas Medal of Excellence, for the Best Paper of IAFSS 2023. IAFSS in the full name of International Association of Fire Safety Science only selects one Philip Thomas Medal Award in every three years. Trully thankful for the selection committee of this medal.
