## Receive IAFSS 2026 Philip Thomas Medal of Excellence

We are thrilled to receive this prestigious award of IAFSS: Philip Thomas Medal of Excellence, for the Best Paper of IAFSS 2023. IAFSS is the international symopsium of International Association of Fire Safety Science, which is held every three years. Trully thankful for the selection committee of this medal.
