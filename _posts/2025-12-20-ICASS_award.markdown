## Best Paper Award on Innovation at ICASS2025 & ISICT 2025 

Our paper presented at ICASS2025 & ISICT 2025 at Singapore was slected as the Best Paper Award on Innovation. The paper title is "AI Powered Hybrid Simulation for modelling global structural behavior in fire without compromising local responses", which is presented by Dr Liming Jiang. The first author of this paper is Dr Ji Wei.
